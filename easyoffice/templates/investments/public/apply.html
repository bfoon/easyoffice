Agreement

How returns work. Each listing is tied to specific items. When they sell, the profit (sale price minus cost and direct expenses) is shared with investors in the listing, in proportion to how much each investor had invested and for how long.

Returns are not guaranteed. Profit depends on sales and can be low or zero in some periods. Where a listing states that investors share losses, a loss-making period reduces your capital.

Your money. Money in your wallet is not invested and earns nothing. Withdrawals are paid after approval to the details you give us. Taking capital out of a listing during its lock-in period carries the stated early-exit fee.

Checks. We may ask for more documents, and may decline or suspend an account to meet legal obligations.

Records. Every movement of your money is recorded and available as a statement in your portal.
