    How a project runs

    Every job, from a new office network to a custom dashboard, follows the same four stages.

    1. We visit your site or meet your team to understand the building, the users and what you need to achieve.

    2. You get a clear plan with equipment list, timeline and a fixed price before any work starts, ready for your procurement process.

    3. Our technicians and developers deliver the work, test it with you, and train your staff to use it.

    4. Maintenance contracts, remote monitoring and a local team to call whenever something needs attention.

    Common questions

    Can’t find your answer? Send us a message.

    Can you work within UN and government procurement processes?

    Yes. We regularly supply and install for UN agencies and government institutions, and provide formal quotations, technical specifications and delivery documentation that fit tender and purchase-order processes.

    Where is EasyOffice hosted, and who owns the data?

    EasyOffice runs on our managed servers or on your own infrastructure. Your organisation owns its data, and we can provide exports at any time.

    Can we start with only a few EasyOffice modules?

    Yes. Most clients start with staff, messaging and files, then add finance, procurement, meetings or POS as they need them. Each module uses the same login and permissions.

    Do you maintain systems after installation?

    We offer maintenance contracts covering scheduled checks, remote monitoring and on-site callouts for security, network and software systems.

    Which areas do you cover?

    We’re based in Banjul and work across the Greater Banjul Area and upcountry. Ask us about projects elsewhere in the region.
